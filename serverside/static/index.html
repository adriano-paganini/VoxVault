Step 2 of 5

Set up VoxVault on your phone

  1. Install the VoxVault Android app and open it.
  2. Accept all requested permissions, including microphone access.
  3. Scan this public-key QR code with your phone's camera, then open the link in VoxVault. The app saves the key automatically.

If the camera cannot open the link, paste the public key into Settings > Encryption Public Key and tap Save.
